# Privacy Policy for Y2K New Tab

Last updated: August 11, 2026

## What this extension does
a new tab page with a Y2K aesthetic.

## Data collection
This extension does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no login, and no server. Everything it does happens locally on your device.

## Local storage
Your settings and saved data are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. They never leave your browser and are cleared when you clear browser data or uninstall the extension.

## Network
This extension makes no network requests of its own. It does not track you, does not read your browsing history, and does not send any data anywhere.
