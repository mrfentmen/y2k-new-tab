# Y2K New Tab

**Tagline:** A Y2K styled start page for your new tab

**Category:** Fun

Step back to the turn of the millennium every time you open a new tab. This page glows with Y2K aesthetic energy: a big live clock in glittery text, stars, the date, and a rotating fact from the early internet days. Sign the guestbook and leave a message for whoever visits your new tab next. It is a playful, colorful homage to the era of dial up, frosted plastic, and absolutely everything being chrome. A fun daily dose of nostalgia for anyone who remembers the year 2000 with affection.
